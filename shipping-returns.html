Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • We deliver all over India.
  • Shipping is a flat per kg, charged for each started kg of your order's total weight. Prices on the website do not include shipping; it is shown at the cart and checkout.
  • Orders are dispatched within hours of the order being placed.
  • The courier partner depends on your area. We share tracking details on WhatsApp after dispatch.

Payment

We accept payment by UPI. After you place your order you will see the UPI payment details. Please send the payment screenshot on WhatsApp so we can confirm your order.

Returns

Returns are accepted only if the product arrives damaged.
  • Please record a continuous unboxing video while opening the parcel. The video is needed as proof of damage.
  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the video within hours of delivery.
  • We will review it and get back to you with a resolution.
  • We cannot accept returns for products that are not damaged, or for damage reported without an unboxing video.
