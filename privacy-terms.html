Privacy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy

When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, order details and, if you choose to give it, your email. We use these only to process, pack, deliver and support your order.

  • Your order details are sent to us by email and WhatsApp so that we can fulfil your order.
  • We share your name, phone number and address with the courier only to deliver your parcel.
  • We do not sell your details. We do not collect or store your UPI PIN or bank details: you pay inside your own UPI app.
  • Your shopping cart is stored in your own browser on your device.

To ask us to delete your details, message us on WhatsApp or email .

Terms of use

  • All prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and do not include shipping, which is shown at checkout.
  • An order is confirmed once we receive your payment. Orders are dispatched within hours.
  • We try to keep product details and photos accurate. Colour and packaging may look slightly different from the photos.
  • Prices and availability may change without notice. If an item you ordered is not available, we will contact you.
  • Shipping and returns follow our Shipping & Returns policy.
